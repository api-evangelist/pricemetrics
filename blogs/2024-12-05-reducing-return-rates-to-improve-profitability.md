Product returns are costly for any business, impacting profitability and disrupting the customer experience. A high return rate can indicate problems with product quality, inaccurate marketing, or issues with the buying process. By reducing return rates, companies can save on reverse logistics, minimize losses, and strengthen customer relationships.
